💡 Relative Dating Principles Reference:

  • Cross-Cutting Relationships: Faults or magma intrusions cutting through rock layers are younger than the layers they cut across.
  • Inclusions: Rock fragments embedded inside a layer are older than the surrounding rock layer.
  • Unconformities: Buried erosional surfaces (like angular unconformities) represent missing geological time.
