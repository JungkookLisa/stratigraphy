💡 Relative Dating Principles Reference:

  • Cross-Cutting Relationships: Faults or intrusions are younger than the rock layers they cut across.
  • Inclusions: Rock fragments inside a layer are older than the surrounding layer.
  • Unconformities: Buried erosional surfaces represent missing geological time.
